GOLHEN OVERVIEW

What is GoldHEN?

GoldHEN is a homebrew-enabling payload for supported PS4 firmware. Compatibility depends on the exact firmware and the payload build used by the host. This dashboard reports browser-detected information only; it cannot verify that a payload is safe or compatible.
